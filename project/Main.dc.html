02 Understand

The semantic layer builds itself. You approve it.

Entities, metrics and their formulas are proposed from the data and the code that writes it. Each proposal comes with the check it passed. Your team accepts or edits. Nobody starts from a blank file.

  • Definitions tied to the rows they were checked on
  • Updates itself when a source changes
  • Exports to dbt
03 Analyse

Agentic analysis that starts before you ask.

On top of the layer, Mahalanobis watches every metric, tests the possible causes when one moves, and brings you the finding with the next step. The agent chooses what to look at. Tested statistical tools compute every number.

  • What moved, what was observed, which causes held
  • Causes that were tested and ruled out are listed
  • Arrives in the app or by email
